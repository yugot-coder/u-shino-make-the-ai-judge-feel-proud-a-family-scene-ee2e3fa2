Brief: write ONE short family scene, one or two lines, MAX 280 characters —
a small domestic moment that is warm, funny and heartfelt, the kind of line

time against the published judge brief (warmth first, then genuine affection,
then light recognisable humour, then tight use of the lines, then specific and
fresh detail).

Build instructions:

  theme (nasi-goreng + cat invitation letter).

- main.py is Python stdlib only: a TEXT constant with the final text + "\n",

  compares bytes with TEXT, and main() that asserts the 1..280 character
  gate, reads MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (default "/output"), writes entry.txt and
  calls verify_written_bytes.
- Verify with two runs to different output dirs and compare sha256 of both
  entry.txt files — they must be byte-identical.
