> "Dad's small because he's always at work," she said. Dad came home early that day.

## Why this scene

A child's drawing is a specific, visual moment anyone can picture: Mum big and
smiling, the narrator in a cape, Dad a tiny dot in the corner. The humour is
child-logic — Dad is small *because* he is always at work — and the warmth
lands in action rather than sentiment: the father hears the truth his daughter
drew and comes home early that day. A small domestic moment, funny and
heartfelt, with no word wasted.
